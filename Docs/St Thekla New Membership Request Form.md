   **PART 4 – STEWARDSHIP**

| Please contact the Treasurer or the Priest if you have any questions. |
| :---- |
|  Our guideline is for each member to set aside 10% of their annual income to St. Thekla Church or other charitable causes. There is a suggested donation (for those who can) of $750 for Individuals and $1200 for Families. In addition, there is a Mandatory annual fee of $220 to meet the Catholicate Day fee, as well as, the Annual Diocesean Fee, however each member should make contributions based on their financial means.  Some members may contribute more, and others may contribute less.  The amount of your contribution will not be publicly available and will only be disclosed on a ‘need-to-know’ basis. You can make an electronic contribution by simply using the following methods: **Zelle:** [sainttheklachurch@gmail.com](mailto:sainttheklachurch@gmail.com) **CashApp:** $SaintTheklaOrthodox **Paypal** (send as Friends and Family to avoid the church incurring additional fees): @SaintTheklaChurch **\*\*Please consider setting up automatic monthly donations at the start of the year. This greatly helps our treasurer managing the church finances and expenses.\*\***   |
